Changelog

Nik follows Semantic Versioning. This page summarises each release; the complete, line-by-line history is CHANGELOG.md in the repository, and every version is published under Releases.

6.9.0 2026-11-21

  • added Recovery to a moment: the database's log is archived and copied off the host, and deploy/restore.sh --to goes back to any moment of the last days.
  • added A standby host that takes the server's place by one command; a server that was replaced refuses to serve.
  • added Work in progress survives a restart: nothing is lost, and a step that changes another system is never repeated by guessing.
  • changed The stack stops politely, and the archive turns on by itself at the upgrade (Upgrading to 6.9).

6.8.0 2026-11-21

  • added The store is encrypted on the disk under a key of its own; the workers no longer mount it.
  • added Secrets in files instead of .env, a Keys page, and a guided change of each key.
  • added The audit log is a signed chain, verified by the server and sent to syslog as it is written.
  • added A Health page with warnings before things break, and metrics for Prometheus behind a token.
  • changed Run deploy/init-env.sh before starting: the server needs its store key (Upgrading to 6.8).

6.7.1 2026-11-21

  • fixed After a restart of the host the server waits for the database instead of staying stopped with "the upgrade was stopped".

6.7.0 2026-11-20

  • added Nik takes its own backups: encrypted, on a schedule, each one restored into a scratch database to prove it, with a copy on a second host over SFTP.
  • added An upgrade is rehearsed on a copy after a snapshot; a failed one leaves the data as it was, and deploy/rollback.sh goes back.
  • added A second factor at sign-in, which can be required of administrators; sessions can be seen and ended.
  • changed Sessions end when unused (30 minutes for administrators); failed sign-ins wait instead of locking (Upgrading to 6.7).

6.6.1 2026-11-20

  • added An open artifact, and a mailbox, has a Close button that returns to the Artifacts page; Back returns to the artifact.

6.6.0 2026-11-20

  • added The rail always goes home, a search bar in the middle of every header, and a question before unsaved work is lost, whatever the way out.
  • added Credentials: a secret or a user name and password kept once in Settings and used by any instance of a connector.
  • added Playbooks work with files: add, download, unpack, analyse and read what the analysis found; three new events; a connector can unpack an archive.
  • added SentinelOne: 142 commands written from the vendor's reference, with files and logs of an endpoint kept as artifacts.
  • changed The data guard judges every look-up: a value with an e-mail address or under your own domains is held until an administrator opens its class (Upgrading to 6.6).
  • fixed A file a playbook keeps is written by the backend: connector downloads and mail attachments failed in the container deployment.

6.5.0 2026-11-20

  • added Connectors handle files: a command keeps what it downloads as an artifact, never opened, and sends a file Nik holds after the data guard judged it.
  • added Expressions decide and count: comparisons, if(…), arithmetic, lists, dates and more filters, the same in the editor and on the server.
  • added The sources of threat intelligence: 51 more commands (586), with downloads and submissions of files, and verdicts written as plain conditions.
  • changed Breaking: the reputation of an indicator is asked of the connectors only. A source needs its pack and an instance; abuse.ch is three sources; the keys entered before move to instances (Upgrading to 6.5).

6.4.0 2026-11-20

  • added Incidents have tags: chips under the name and in the quick view, a column and a chip in the list, tag: in queries, two playbook actions (Tags). Every field of an incident can be queried, the custom ones too.
  • added Nik's menu on every page: the right button opens it everywhere and the browser's own does not appear; cut, copy and paste in text fields, the actions of what is under the pointer, and a general section.
  • added The playbook editor has a right-click menu and lists the fields inside what a node gives; Tidy up never draws a line over a node.
  • added Fetching is set in the instance: how often, the first fetch, a look-back and Reset last run, with the classifier and the mappers as tabs of the same form (Fetching).
  • added Content packs in bulk, with a filter by tag; a pack in use is not uninstalled.
  • added Administrators are told when a worker is down and when it is back; a playbook node scans an address with the isolated browser (Scan an address).
  • changed Breaking: the old role and scopes of accounts and API keys are removed; forward and reply of the Mail connector are judged by the data guard (Upgrading to 6.4).

6.3.0 2026-11-19

  • added Compare a branch with main before a push: the two graphs side by side, what was added, changed and removed marked on them and listed, and the fields of a node that changed.
  • changed A branch runs only in the debugger: nothing else starts it, in the editor or on the server.
  • added Mail connector: get-attachments keeps the attachments of a message as files of the incident; forward carries them when asked and the data guard lets them; a message the listener makes an incident from is kept as mail evidence of it.

6.2.0 2026-11-19

  • added The list of incidents is a page, with a quick view that opens over it at one click, saved filters (personal, shared by an administrator, one default each), chips that write the query and a choice of columns (The list of incidents).
  • added The pages of a type: a type orders and hides the tabs of its incidents and adds pages of its own; it chooses what the Close dialog asks and what the quick view shows.
  • added Script buttons on the pages of an incident: text, colours, a script, and arguments asked when pressed or filled from the incident with {{ field }}, once for each value of a field that holds several. Two more widgets: a chart of a dashboard narrowed to the incident, and linked incidents.
  • added A dialog asks which system an incident is about on the pages that depend on it, instead of showing Windows paths.
  • changed The side panel of incidents is gone; playbook buttons are script buttons.

6.1.0 2026-11-19

  • added The sources of threat intelligence are connectors. Eleven packs in the catalogue (VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB, urlscan.io, MalwareBazaar, URLhaus, ThreatFox, AlienVault OTX, GreyNoise, Shodan, Google Safe Browsing, RDAP), with every command their vendors document: 535 blocks for the playbook editor. The commands of a paid plan are marked premium.
  • added Several keys for one instance, and how they take turns: one request each, the same until it is used up, the least used, or the first with the others in reserve; limits for each key, and what a command does when all are used up (Several keys). An instance may also keep answers for some hours.
  • added Try it on each command of a connector opens the Playground with its line; a connector lists the other hosts its commands reach (more_hosts); the filters urlencode and base64url.
  • changed The automatic look-ups use the keys of the instances of a source's connector, beside the keys of Settings.
  • fixed urlscan.io: a search result judged malicious is read in either shape of the answer.

6.0.0 2026-11-19

  • added A catalogue of content packs. What Nik knows about a product of a vendor is a pack of its own (its connector, mappers, classifier, playbooks, types and dashboards), listed by vendor in Settings → Automation → Content packs and installed from there. The index of the catalogue is signed and each pack is checked against it (The catalogue).
  • added Uninstalling a pack removes what it gave, except what was changed here or is in use, which stays with its reason; instances and credentials go only when asked.
  • changed The connectors of vendors are not inside Nik any more. A Nik that is upgraded keeps every one it has, written down as the pack it is; a new Nik starts with Nik built-in alone.
  • changed The API says instances where it said connections (/api/automation/instances, instanceId), and IoC library is called Indicators.
  • removed status:!closed is no longer read: "is not" is written before the field (!status:closed), as since 5.0.
  • changed Upgrading: see Upgrading to 6.0.

4.7.0 2026-11-19

  • added Script signatures can be edited in Settings → Rule sets → Script signatures: one Nik ships is turned off, changed and put back as it shipped; your own are added beside them. A pattern is tried on long hostile texts before it is saved, and matched with a time limit.
  • added A list of indicators keeps the history of its checks: what the last check changed, verdict by verdict, and the checks before it. Tag… adds and removes tags on everything selected or kept by a filter.
  • added An artifact can be given an expiry. Nothing has one unless someone sets it; on that day the artifact is deleted as by hand, with an event a week before and a line of the audit log. One an open incident references is put off by a week.
  • added Mailboxes travel in an export of artifacts, with their index and, on request, their file; and the page of a mailbox says how far its reading is.
  • changed An Outlook PST is converted on disk, on a volume of the document worker (pst-work), and the largest accepted goes from 500 MB to 4 GB (Upgrading to 4.7).

4.6.0 2026-11-19

  • added Dashboards. Pages of widgets, each a question asked of what Nik holds and drawn as a number, a trend, bars, a donut, a list or a table, on a grid you arrange. A value opens the objects behind it; a dashboard is yours until it is shared, and whoever sees it sees only what their own roles let them read. Four ship with Nik, and any can be made a PDF (Dashboards).
  • added Counting in the query language: POST /api/query/aggregate groups and measures what a query finds, over a time range and against the period before. Runs, approvals and tasks can be queried (Counting and measuring).
  • added Content packs. Connectors, playbooks and their scripts, types and fields, mappers, lists, dashboards and pre-processing rules travel as one .nikpack file. A pack is inspected before anything changes, item by item, with the difference of what it would update, and applied whole or not at all. What Nik ships is listed as packs (Content packs).
  • added A debugger for playbooks: a run that stops before its nodes, shows what each is about to be given, takes other arguments, skips a node, runs one again, and holds what goes outside unless one node is let through (The debugger).

4.5.0 2026-11-18

  • added Fetching. A connection can bring the alerts of its product in as incidents: turned on for one connection at a time, read at an interval, each alert taken once, with limits that pause a connection that floods. Settings → Automation → Fetch status shows how each goes (Fetching).
  • added Classifiers and mappers, with an editor that tries them on a sample: which type of incident an item becomes, which of its values go to which fields and to the context, and what goes back (Classifiers and mappers).
  • added Pre-processing rules: what is fetched can be dropped, linked to an incident that is the same, closed as a duplicate or given to a script, before it becomes an incident.
  • added Mirroring. An incident and a ticket or an issue of another product kept in step, in, out or both ways, with their comments; what a person set in Nik is kept unless the mapper says otherwise (Mirroring).
  • added The shipped connectors fetch and mirror: ServiceNow, Jira Cloud and Data Center, SentinelOne EDR and SIEM bring alerts in, each with its mapper; ServiceNow and Jira are mirrored (the connectors Nik ships). None was run against a real system.
  • added A connector file says how it fetches and mirrors with fetch, schema, ext_fields and mirror; two filters, severity and regex_extract, and pairs in map.

4.4.0 2026-11-17

  • added Tasks and questions in playbooks. A Task node gives a person something to do, a Question node asks them to choose or to fill a small form, with an edge for each answer. Either is for a user, a role, or the owner of the incident; a task past its due time is overdue and stays to do.
  • added The Work plan of an incident: what its playbooks do, step by step under the phases they name, as a list or as the drawing of the playbook, with its tasks. My tasks, on the rail, is what waits for you across every incident.
  • added SLA timers: a type of incident says how long its incidents are allowed to be assigned, contained and closed, by severity and in business hours if wanted; a timer past its time marks the incident, tells its owner and starts the playbooks that wait for sla.breached.
  • added Notifications: a bell on the rail with the tasks given to you, the approvals that wait, the mentions (@you) and the SLAs past their time.
  • added Lists, in Settings: what features and playbooks read by a name, edited, imported and exported; playbooks read and write them and ask | in_list('name') (Lists).
  • changed What Settings kept as fixed lists (the IoC allowlist, your domains, the trusted mail servers, the suspicious keywords, the business hours, the hosts every playbook may reach) are lists now, moved as they were (Upgrading to 4.4).

4.3.0 2026-11-17
